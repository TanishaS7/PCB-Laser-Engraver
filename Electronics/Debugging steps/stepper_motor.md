# Stepper Motor Debugging

## Problem: Motor Vibrates But Does Not Rotate

### Possible Causes
- Wrong coil pairing
- Insufficient current
- Loose driver connection

### Diagnostic Steps
1. Verify motor coil pairs using multimeter (check contuinity, the coils together will show contuinity)
2. Check driver current limit
3. Verify driver orientation on CNC shield


## Problem: Missed Steps

### Possible Causes
- Low power supply current
- Belt slipping
- Excessive acceleration and speed

### Fixes
- Reduced acceleration
- Increased belt tension
- Improved power supply stability
